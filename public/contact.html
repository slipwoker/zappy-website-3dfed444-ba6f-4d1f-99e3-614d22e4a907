FAQ

Quick clarity on common questions about SJohnson games, builds, and collaboration.

How do I get the latest build?

All current builds are available on our itch.io page. Sign up for our newsletter to get notified when new versions drop, or follow us on social media for instant updates.

Do you provide press notes?

Absolutely! Press kits with screenshots, logos, fact sheets, and trailers are available on request. Just shoot us an email and we'll send over everything you need.

Are the games streamer-friendly?

100% streamer-friendly. No copyright strikes, no content ID issues. Stream away and tag us—we love seeing gameplay content from the community!

What platforms are supported?

Currently Windows PC (Steam, itch.io) and select titles on Linux. We're exploring mobile game developers tools for future releases. Check individual game pages for specific platform details.

Can I collaborate?

We're always open to creative partnerships! Whether you're a composer, pixel artist, or fellow video game developer, reach out via our contact form with your portfolio and ideas.

Prefer to browse first?

Check out my game projects and see what I've been building. You can always come back to get in touch when you're ready.
