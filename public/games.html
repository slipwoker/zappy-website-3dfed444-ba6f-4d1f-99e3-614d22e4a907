Games by SJohnson

Every game I make is a love letter to the scrappy, chaotic energy of early-2000s indie dev culture—retro arcade vibes, weird humor, and fast iteration. No pretense, just pure experimentation and fun. I build small, strange, and memorable experiences that don't take themselves too seriously.

Build Rhythm

Our development process thrives on frequent iteration and community feedback. Each milestone brings new features, polish, and player-driven improvements. We believe in shipping early, listening closely, and evolving our games alongside the community that plays them.

Prototype Drop

Early-stage build released to core testers. Rough edges everywhere, but the core loop is playable. We gather initial reactions and identify what works (and what doesn't).Target: Q2 2024

Playtest Weekend

Open beta weekend with expanded access. Stress-test servers, collect metrics, and watch players break things in creative ways. Community feedback drives the next sprint.Target: This Summer

Polish Pass

Refinement phase focusing on UI/UX improvements, bug fixes, and performance optimization. We tighten animations, balance gameplay, and squash the last major issues before launch.Target: Q3 2024

Next Milestone

Full release candidate with all features locked in. Final QA sweep, marketing push, and community hype-building. Launch day is around the corner—time to ship this thing.Target: This Fall
